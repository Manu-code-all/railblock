# Package 3 — Demo and pitch

**Owner: Member 3 · Due Friday 5 September**

You present on the day. Everything else the team builds is judged through the
five minutes you deliver.

Three deliverables: the demo script, the slides, and the Q&A preparation. The
Q&A is the one most teams skip and the one that decides close calls.

---

## Deliverable A — the five-minute demo

Fill in `demo-script.md`. The shape that works:

| | Beat | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The problem — three departments, no coordination | 0:45 |
| 2 | Live: a department submits a request | 0:30 |
| 3 | Live: the controller solves — proven optimal | 0:45 |
| 4 | Live: click a block, see *why* it is there | 1:00 |
| 5 | Live: the overloaded corridor names its conflict | 1:00 |
| 6 | Publish the block order, show the export | 0:30 |
| 7 | Impact and honest scope | 0:30 |

Beats 4 and 5 are the ones nobody else will have. Give them room.

**Rehearse on the actual laptop, with the actual data.** A demo that works on
your machine and not the presentation machine is the most common way teams lose
a round.

## Deliverable C — judge Q&A

Fill in `qa-prep.md`. Write an answer for every question there, in your own
words, and rehearse the hard ones out loud with the team.

The single most likely question is *"how is this different from any scheduling
app?"* Our answer is that a scheduling app produces **an** answer; ours produces
**the provably best** answer, and when none exists it tells you exactly which
requests are in conflict. Nobody should fumble that one.
